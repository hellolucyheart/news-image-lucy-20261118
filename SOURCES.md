# Sources — the news of 18 November 2026

The poster sums up the five largest world-news stories of Wednesday,
18 November 2026, as reported that day by multiple independent outlets:

1. **Shenzhen summit (Asia-Pacific)** — Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin met in
   Shenzhen ahead of a planned trilateral summit with Donald Trump; Xi
   pledged to stand against trade protectionism.
   Reported by AFP / Associated Press via Open Chronicle's Global News Brief
   and Just Security's Early Edition.
2. **Gulf escalation (Middle East)** — The United States struck a
   Revolutionary Guard missile site on Iran's Qeshm Island in the Strait of
   Hormuz; Iran answered with drone strikes on Bahrain and Kuwait, and
   Houthi drones targeted Saudi pipeline pumping stations.
   Reported by Reuters / Wall Street Journal via Just Security's Early
   Edition and One Turn News.
3. **Kerch Bridge (Ukraine)** — Ukraine struck the Kerch Bridge to Crimea
   for a second consecutive day; part of the road span collapsed into the
   strait.
   Reported by One Turn News and Horizon.
4. **US Senate (United States)** — Democrat Mary Peltola defeated incumbent
   Dan Sullivan in Alaska, giving Democrats a projected 56–44 majority in
   the incoming Senate.
   Reported by the Associated Press via Open Chronicle.
5. **Ebola (Africa)** — The Ebola outbreak in the Democratic Republic of
   Congo more than doubled in three months, passing 11,000 cases and 5,400
   deaths, with children under five hit hardest.
   Reported by the UN via One Turn News and Horizon.
